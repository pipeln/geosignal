How this works

  1. Crawl. The homepage plus internal links (2 clicks deep), respecting robots.txt.
  2. Score. 34 documented local checks across entity, on-page, linking, technical and trust.
  3. Explain. Every pass and failure carries the mechanism and the Google source, so the report teaches as it audits.
  4. Cross-check. Optionally ask 1–3 small local models; results are curated by agreement, not by vibes.

Not affiliated with Google. Scores estimate ranking readiness, not a ranking position.
